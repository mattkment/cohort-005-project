# Cadence

Cadence sells online courses. This glossary fixes the language used for buying a course and getting the money back.

## Language

**Purchase**:
A single payment for access to one course.

**Student**:
A person who buys and learns from courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Learner, buyer

**Refund**:
A Student's request to get their money back for a Purchase, which Cadence's team reviews by hand. It is either Requested, Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancel, cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Pending Chargeback

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund that has been submitted and is waiting on Cadence's team. Informally "pending".
_Avoid_: Pending (as a term of its own)

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund that Cadence's team has accepted, with money returned to the Student.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund that Cadence's team has rejected, with no money returned to the Student.
